# Content & SEO Strategy for Dr. Krishna Koirala ENT

To dominate Google and AI search results (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) and maintain a high-conversion website, we recommend the following content strategy:

## 1. Local SEO & AI Visibility
*   **Google Business Profile:** Keep your Nayabazar ENT Care Center profile updated with high-resolution clinic photos and encourage patients to leave detailed reviews. AI models heavily weight local business data.
*   **Consistent NAP:** Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number are identical across the website, Facebook, and Google Maps.
*   **Video Schema:** For every YouTube/TikTok video you embed, we have included `VideoObject` schema. Continue posting videos that answer common patient questions (e.g., "What to do for ear pain?").

## 2. Blog / Knowledge Center (Multilingual)
Aim for 1-2 articles per month in both English and Nepali. Focus on "Long-Tail" keywords:
*   *Topic:* Sinusitis treatment in Pokhara.
*   *Topic:* Recovering from Thyroid surgery at MCOMS.
*   *Topic:* Why do children get ear infections in winter? (बालबालिकामा कान पाक्ने समस्या)

## 3. Social Media Integration
*   **TikTok/YouTube Shorts:** Continue the high-value educational clips. These are excellent for building "E-E-A-T" (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness).
*   **Patient Stories:** With consent, share brief testimonials or "Case of the Week" to build community trust.

## 4. Technical Maintenance
*   **Performance:** The site is built with Next.js 15 for speed. Ensure images are always uploaded in WebP format to maintain 95+ Lighthouse scores.
*   **CMS Updates:** We recommend integrating Sanity.io or Payload CMS in Phase 2 for easier blog management without touching code.

## 5. Paid Search (Optional)
*   **Google Ads:** Targeted ads for "Best ENT in Pokhara" or "Thyroid surgeon Nepal" during peak seasons can provide an immediate boost in appointments.
